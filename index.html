THE LAST PRESENT

Just for a minute. Don't get used to it.

Dear Aleeza,

Happy birthday. 💗 I could've written something normal, like “hope you have a great day,” but you know me. I apparently have to turn everything into a whole production.

I think one of my favourite things about you is how different you are once someone actually gets to know you. You can seem so cold and mysterious at first — and, okay, sometimes you ARE very serious — but then you start talking and suddenly we're discussing absolutely anything. Books, cats, the future, ridiculous imaginary situations. You always have something interesting to say.

And I notice the little things. When you remember my birthday at the right time. When you remember that I love flowers. When you say thank you or tell me something meant a lot to you, even though I know you're not always the most expressive person. Those moments mean a lot to me too.

I miss the way we used to talk so easily. I miss the school days and the random conversations, and how comfortable I felt around you. Being in different schools is strange. Sometimes I wish I could just go back to one ordinary day when we were all together, without even knowing we'd miss it this much later.

But I don't want our friendship to become something that only exists in old chats and memories. I want more conversations, more silly jokes, more updates about our days, more outings, and hopefully a proper London adventure someday. Double-decker bus included, obviously.

I hope you know you don't have to carry every stressful thing on your own. Please take care of yourself. Eat, drink water (I know, a revolutionary concept for the former alien), rest, and be kind to yourself. You deserve good friends, peaceful days, and people who make you feel safe being exactly who you are.

May Allah protect you, bless you with a long, healthy, happy life, ease your worries, guide you toward what is best for you, and grant you success in your exams and everything you dream of doing. Ameen. I hope this year brings you so much goodness.

And one last thing: please don't forget me, okay? I know life takes people to different places, but I really hope we keep finding our way back to each other. I'll always be grateful that we became friends.

Happy birthday again, Aleeza. You're genuinely gorgeous, wonderfully strange, and very, very loved. 🖤🌹

With love,
Zoha 💗
